Minecraft — Still a classic.

Author: Alex Sharma

Rating: 9.0/10

Minecraft remains one of the most flexible games available. Its combination of exploration, building, survival, and creativity gives players almost unlimited ways to play.

Valorant — Competitive but Demanding

Author: Riya Patel

Rating: 8.5/10

Valorant rewards communication, strategy, and precise aim. The learning curve can be difficult for new players, but improving your skills makes the game very rewarding.

Hollow Knight — Beautiful and Challenging

Author: Arjun Mehta

Rating: 9.2/10

Hollow Knight combines atmospheric exploration with challenging combat and a beautifully designed world. Its difficulty can be frustrating, but exploration and progression make the experience worthwhile.
